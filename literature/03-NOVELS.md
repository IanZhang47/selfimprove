These regional groups preserve the original comparison sets rather than assert pure genre boundaries. Borges’s collections are short fiction; Ernaux’s *The Years* belongs to autobiographical writing; Primo Levi’s testimony also appears in [Essays and literary nonfiction](06-ESSAYS-AND-NONFICTION.md); and Goethe’s *Faust* is verse drama. Historical attributions and publication dates should be checked when they become part of a research question.

|---|---|---|

## Russian

A character-name glossary and translator’s introduction can help. Check what your exact edition includes.

Tolstoy (*War and Peace*, *Anna Karenina*) · Dostoevsky (*Crime and Punishment*, *The Brothers Karamazov*, *Notes from Underground*, *Demons*) · Gogol (*Dead Souls*, *The Overcoat*) · Turgenev (*Fathers and Sons*) · Bulgakov (*The Master and Margarita*) · Grossman (*Life and Fate*) · Nabokov (*Lolita*, *Pale Fire*)

> **Translation candidates:** Pevear & Volokhonsky; Maude for Tolstoy; Briggs for *War and Peace*; Katz or Ready for relevant Dostoevsky works. Read the same passage in two available editions and check the actual title each translator translated. This is a comparison set, not a claim of scholarly consensus about a winner.
