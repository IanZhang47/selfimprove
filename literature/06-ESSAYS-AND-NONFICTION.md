# Essays and Literary Nonfiction

[Literature guide](00-READING-GUIDE.md) · [My study plan](../01-MY-STUDY-PLAN.md) · [Subject map](../03-SUBJECT-MAP.md)

**Catalog status:** Candidate unless a linked source record identifies a specific check. [How to use these labels](00-READING-GUIDE.md#source-status).

Read for form, voice, argument, and experience while also evaluating factual claims. An essay, testimony, or memoir can be artistically important and still require corroboration, historical context, or correction. Literary quality does not establish empirical reliability.

|---|---|---|
