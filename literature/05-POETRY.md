For poetry, inspect line breaks, indentation, stanza spacing, and notes in the actual edition. See [access and formats](../reference/06-ACCESS-AND-FORMATS.md).

Try silent reading, reading aloud, and recordings to notice different features. A facing-language edition can help when you have some source-language knowledge or want to study layout; it cannot establish translation accuracy merely by showing similar line lengths. Audio or alternative accessible formats are valid routes.

## English

Chaucer (*Canterbury Tales* — Riverside edition) · Shakespeare's *Sonnets* · Donne and the Metaphysicals · Milton (*Paradise Lost*) · Blake (*Songs of Innocence and of Experience*) · Wordsworth · Keats (the odes and the letters together) · Dickinson · Whitman (*Song of Myself*) · Hopkins · Yeats · Eliot (*The Waste Land*, *Four Quartets*) · Frost · Stevens · Auden · Bishop · Larkin · Plath · Walcott (*Omeros*) · Heaney

**Anthologies:** *The Norton Anthology of Poetry*; Heaney & Hughes, *The Rattle Bag* (a candidate selection to sample).

## Chinese

《诗经》 · 屈原《楚辞》 · 陶渊明 · 王维 · 李白 · 杜甫 · 白居易 · 李商隐 · 李清照 · 苏轼 · 辛弃疾

**Use the language route that supports understanding.** See [Chinese and bilingual routes](10-CHINESE-AND-BILINGUAL-ROUTES.md) for Chinese notes and diagnostics. Owen, Hinton, and Watson remain translation candidates; Stephen Owen’s *An Anthology of Chinese Literature* can supply works and context, including for bilingual comparison.

## Japanese

*Man'yōshū* · *Kokinshū* · Bashō (*The Narrow Road to the Deep North* — Sato or Hamill) · Buson · Issa · Yosano Akiko

## Persian, Arabic, Urdu

Rumi (Nicholson or Jawid Mojaddedi as direct-translation candidates; distinguish Coleman Barks’ interpretive versions and their intermediary sources from direct translations) · Hafez (Dick Davis) · Saadi · Khayyám (FitzGerald’s *Rubáiyát* is a free poetic adaptation; he studied Persian, so do not describe it as work by someone without the source language. See [S2](12-SOURCE-REGISTER.md#s2)) · the *Mu'allaqāt* · Ghalib · Faiz Ahmed Faiz

## Greek & Latin

Sappho (Anne Carson, *If Not, Winter* — fragments with the gaps left visible) · Pindar · Catullus · Horace · Cavafy (Mendelsohn)

## Continental European

## South Asian

Kālidāsa (*Meghadūta*) · Kabir (Hess & Singh) · Mirabai · Tagore (*Gitanjali*)
