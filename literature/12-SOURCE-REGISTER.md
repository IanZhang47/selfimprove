Existing [checked-library records](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md) retain their stated scope. ## S1

**Source-checked**

Kelly J. Mays, [*The Norton Introduction to Literature*, 15th edition](https://seagull.wwnorton.com/introliterature): literary reading and writing instruction; inspect which full or portable version you choose

## S2

**Correction sources**

[Cambridge University Library’s FitzGerald archive description](https://archivesearch.lib.cam.ac.uk/agents/people/6043) identifies Persian translations; the scholarly [Encyclopaedia Iranica account](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/fitzgerald/) documents his Persian study and free handling of texts. These sources correct the biography; they do not endorse a particular *Rubáiyát* edition

## S3

**Source-checked / comparison context**

[Royall Tyler’s *The Tale of Genji*](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.ca/books/297312/the-tale-of-genji-by-murasaki-shikibu-edited-and-translated-by-royall-tyler/9780143039495) identifies the translator and edition. Tyler’s [lecture on translating *Genji*](https://keene.center/download_files/tyler-sen-lecture-notes-2009.pdf) provides a translator’s account of choices and predecessors; it is one informed perspective, not a neutral ranking

## S4

**Source-checked**

Norton’s [Carole Satyamurti, *Mahabharata: A Modern Retelling*](https://wwnorton.co.uk/books/9780393081756-mahabharata): narrative retelling in verse; do not label it a complete direct Sanskrit translation

## S5

**Source-checked**

Chicago’s [van Buitenen volume 1](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/M/bo5948252.html) identifies its scope as book 1; the university’s [account of the translation project](https://ahd.uchicago.edu/news/epic-endeavor) specifies that he translated books 1–5 before his death. Check other volumes and translators separately

## S6

**Source-checked**

[Jorge Luis Borges, *Collected Fictions*, translated by Andrew Hurley](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/330909/collected-fictions-by-jorge-luis-borges-translated-by-andrew-hurley/): identifies the collection containing the *Ficciones* material; formats and abridgment must be checked separately

## S7

**Source-checked**

Chicago’s [*The Journey to the West*, revised edition, volume 1, translated and edited by Anthony C. Yu](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/J/bo12079590.html), and [volume 4](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/J/bo12893528.html): the four-volume translation route, not a one-volume abridgment

## S8

**Access guidance**

[Project Gutenberg copyright guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/help/copyright.html): rights depend on the text and jurisdiction; a catalog does not guarantee the availability of a desired modern translation or annotations

## Review boundary

This revision retains the original literary and translation leads, removes unsupported rankings and numerical authority claims, corrects specific translation/scope errors, replaces compulsory formats and assumed language ability with edition checks, and adds a progression from close reading to specialist study. It does not claim to have verified every retained title, translation, attribution, date, or annotation. Promote a Candidate entry to Source-checked only after adding its exact official or scholarly evidence and the date of review.
