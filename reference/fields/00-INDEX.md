## How to interpret this index

Retrieved 9 September 2026 from the [official NCES CIP 2020 browser](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/cipcode/browse.aspx?y=56).

The source contains **50 family codes, 464 branch codes, and 2173 detailed program codes**. Excluding reserved placeholders, those counts are **48, 457, and 2143**, respectively. Reserved codes are retained for source fidelity; the 30 reserved detailed entries have no learning checkbox.

This is an official classification of instructional programs, used here as a coverage checklist. It is not an official curriculum, an inventory of every possible human idea, or a claim that every listed program is an academic major. It includes nondegree skills and professional training. General, interdisciplinary, and “other” entries overlap more specific entries; do not count them as separate degrees to complete.

The labels and codes below are reproduced from NCES, a U.S. federal source. Classification provides categories for reporting instructional programs. An available category does not establish actual program provision or validate theories or practices under that label. Entry counts are a coverage aid, not a measure of the proportion of human knowledge learned: entries overlap and vary greatly in scope.

**How to use:** a checked box means you have demonstrated introductory orientation to that field: explain its main questions, identify its major branches, name an appropriate source, and state important limits. It does not mean mastery or qualification. Track further stages separately using the template in [the lifetime curriculum](../../02-HOW-TO-STUDY.md).

**Resource status:** detailed book routes are not yet verified for every program. Use the [source-checked starting library](../02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md) for its stated scopes. This curriculum distinguishes source-checked entries from candidates; it does not claim an audited bibliography for every program.

**Cross-cultural coverage:** CIP is a U.S. program taxonomy. Supplement regional history, languages, religion, arts, Indigenous knowledge, and institutions with scholarship from the traditions being studied. [UNESCO ISCED-F](https://www.uis.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2025/04/international-standard-classification-of-education-fields-of-education-and-training-2013-detailed-field-descriptions-2015-en.pdf) supplies an international cross-check.
