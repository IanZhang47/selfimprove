# How to choose and check sources

[Home](../00-START-HERE.md) · [My plan](../01-MY-STUDY-PLAN.md) · [Subject map](../03-SUBJECT-MAP.md)

Choose sources for relevant expertise, evidence, clarity and correction practices. No author, publisher or list can certify every claim forever. These are the shared standards for the whole folder.

[Status labels](#status) · [Evidence by field](#evidence) · [Book record](#book-record) · [Claim record](#claim-record)

<a id="status"></a>
## Status labels

- **Source-checked:** the stated identity, edition, scope or access was checked against an official author, publisher, university or institutional source. Read the scope note: this does not mean every assertion was independently verified.
- **Candidate:** a retained or proposed reading whose exact edition, subject fit, current evidence or access still needs review before it becomes the core of a study route. Candidate does not mean false or poor.
- **Claim assessment:** separately record well-supported findings, qualified models, contested claims, interpretations and value judgments, with reasons. A whole book does not receive a blanket truth certificate.

A source check and a judgment about a claim are different. The [checked library](02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md) records the scope of completed checks; unmarked catalog recommendations remain Candidates.

<a id="evidence"></a>
## Match evidence to the field

Check the claim and the book's role. A university press, degree, famous name, or large reference list is useful context, but none is a guarantee.

| Kind of knowledge | Strong basis for learning | What to keep separate |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics and formal logic | Explicit definitions, valid arguments, proof checks, examples and counterexamples, usable problems and corrections | A proof within assumptions versus a model's applicability to the world |
| Empirical science | Methods appropriate to the question; good measurement; converging evidence; careful synthesis; uncertainty and correction | Established findings, useful models, preliminary results, and speculation |
| Statistics and research methods | Assumptions, study design, identification, diagnostics, sensitivity, reproducible analysis | Association, prediction, and causal inference |
| Engineering and computing | Foundational theory; specifications; reproducible implementations; testing; failure analysis; relevant current standards | A working demonstration, a robust system, and professional qualification |
| History and archaeology | Source provenance, context, source criticism, material evidence, and scholarship addressing alternatives | Documented events, reconstructed explanations, and disputed interpretation |
| Philosophy and ethics | Clearly stated arguments, premises, definitions, counterarguments, and engagement with serious alternatives | Factual premises, logical implications, and value commitments |
| Literature and the arts | Reliable texts and editions, careful translation, evidence from the work, historical context, informed criticism, making and performance | Textual facts, interpretations, evaluative preferences, and artistic experience |
| Law and institutions | Applicable primary legal sources, authoritative interpretation, jurisdiction and date | Law as it stands, a commentator's explanation, and an argument about what it should be |
| Medicine and public health | Foundational science, well-conducted research syntheses, applicable current guidance and supervised education for clinical work | General understanding, a research result, and a decision about an individual patient |
| Social and practical skills | Research where appropriate, transparent practice guidance, realistic exercises, feedback, and sensitivity to context | An observed behavior, a plausible technique, and a universal effectiveness claim |

For empirical questions, prefer a strong synthesis when you lack context. A single primary study is not automatically better evidence than a careful textbook or review. A systematic review can itself have weak inputs or methods. Include well-supported alternatives without treating every opposing claim as equally credible.

Do not blacklist an entire author merely because one chapter is disputed. Record the specific issue, its evidence, whether it affects the book's main conclusion, and any correction or response.

<a id="book-record"></a>
## Before using a book as your main text

Before making a book the core of a route, record:

- Exact title, author/editor, edition or version, publication date, and official link.
- Subject and level; intended audience; relevant expertise and editorial process.
- What it covers, what it omits, and prerequisites you actually need.
- Exercises, solutions, datasets, primary-source collections, recordings, or other ways to check your understanding.
- Errata and relevant critiques; date-sensitive chapters; important live disagreements.
- Access: authorized web text, PDF, ebook, print, or library. Assess the actual edition on your device.
- Review status: **identity/scope checked**, **sample content assessed**, or **core route tested**. These statuses are not interchangeable.

The new starting library has identity and scope checks against official sources. It has not had every assertion independently audited. That distinction is especially important for a curriculum centered on reliability.

A useful example is Axler's *Linear Algebra Done Right*: the author provides [both an authorized text](https://linear.axler.net/) and [an errata page](https://www.linear.axler.net/LADRErrata4e.html). The correction history is part of the resource, not a reason to pretend any edition is flawless.

## Keep a short claim record

| Claim or question | Source and date | Evidence and assumptions | Confidence and disagreement | What would change my view? | Review trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |  |  |  |

Use labels such as well established, supported with qualifications, disputed, preliminary, or interpretive/value-based only with reasons. Avoid numerical confidence that merely makes an impression look precise.

For a disputed claim, identify the exact passage, the relevant evidence or critique, and a substantive response or correction. The [claim-review register](05-CLAIM-REVIEW.md) preserves questions from the earlier files; questions are not findings that the books are false.

When using law, health information, financial rules, standards, security advice or software instructions, check the date, jurisdiction/version and context relevant to your task. Stable definitions and mathematical results still require their assumptions and correct reasoning.
