## Build a route for an individual major

1. Locate the specific program and adjacent branches in the field index. Compare suitable current university or professional curricula, rather than assuming a book title establishes completeness.
2. List prerequisites and core competencies; map each to chapters, exercises and required practice. Mark every uncovered component explicitly.
3. Compare two plausible core texts using samples, exercises, teaching use, errata and scope. Choose the one that supports learning, without rewarding fame or unnecessary difficulty.
4. Add complementary laboratory, studio, field, language, team or professional work. Use suitable expert feedback for tasks where self-marking is insufficient.
5. Assess the chosen competence before widening the route. Keep Stage 4 maintenance active; reading primary papers does not end the need for bibliographies, instruction or correction.

After choosing a route, record its next outcome in [My Study Plan](../01-MY-STUDY-PLAN.md).
