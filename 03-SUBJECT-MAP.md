## Read the catalog columns

**Orientation / perspective** helps you locate a topic. **Core / practice** supports systematic study. **Specialist / reference / further study** offers narrower, deeper or supporting work. Columns describe a possible use; they are not guarantees of difficulty or a compulsory reading order.

Unless an entry explicitly says **Source-checked**, it is a **Candidate**. The [source standards](reference/01-SOURCE-STANDARDS.md) explain the labels. A dagger (†) points to the [claim-review register](reference/05-CLAIM-REVIEW.md).

Choose one resource or chapter sequence, check its prerequisites, and put the next outcome in [My Study Plan](01-MY-STUDY-PLAN.md). Additional fields remain available throughout your lifetime; the map is a place to return to, not a list to finish in one pass.
