## Prepare and practise

Separate general health literacy, biomedical science, clinical knowledge and assessed clinical practice. Body systems and chemistry support physiology/pharmacology; statistics and study design support appraisal of treatment claims. Reference books are consulted selectively and can require substantial prior study. **Working evidence:** interpret absolute risks and study limitations; demonstrate practical tasks in suitable training. **Checked foundations:** [Anatomy and Physiology 2e](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R43](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r43)**; public reference [Merck Manual Consumer Version](https://www.merckmanuals.com/) **Source-checked [R44](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r44)**; first-aid practice [First Aid/CPR/AED Participant's Manual, r.25](https://www.redcross.org/store/first-aid-cpr-aed-participants-manual/754200.html) **Source-checked [R48](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r48)**. Use current topic guidance for decisions.
