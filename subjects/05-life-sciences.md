## Prepare and practise

Build general biology with chemistry and quantitative reasoning before molecular, physiological or population specialisms. Genetics and evolution connect many branches; field and organismal knowledge require observation beyond molecular textbooks. Case narratives can raise questions but do not establish prevalence or a general mechanism. **Working evidence:** explain a mechanism with its evidence, analyse a dataset, and complete an appropriate field or laboratory exercise. **Checked entry alternative:** [Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R12](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r12)**.
