## Prepare and practise

Before algorithms and systems, establish programming fluency and discrete reasoning; probability, linear algebra and calculus precede substantial machine learning. Systems topics also need practical programming, debugging and measurement. NLP is a specialist branch of linguistics/computing, not a substitute for a general linguistics core. **Working evidence:** build and explain a tested program, analyse its limitations, and reproduce a small published result where appropriate. Refresh tool-, security- and model-dependent material when using it.
