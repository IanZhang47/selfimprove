## Prepare and practise

Language study, language acquisition and interpersonal communication have different outcomes. For a language, combine comprehensible material, grammar as needed, listening or signing, output and corrective feedback. When reading Krashen's Input Hypothesis, identify the actual theoretical claim and examine the evidence; do not reduce language learning to one universal slogan. For communication, practise listening, clear requests, disagreement and repair with another person. **Working evidence:** a revised writing sample, a conversation with feedback, or a language task at a declared level. **Checked social core:** [Looking Out, Looking In, 16th ed.](https://www.cengage.com/c/etextbook-looking-out-looking-in-16e-adler-proctor/9780357658307/) **Source-checked [R31](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r31)**.
