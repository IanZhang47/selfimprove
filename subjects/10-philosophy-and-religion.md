## Prepare and practise

Acquire the terms needed to reconstruct an argument before judging it. Philosophy, religious history, theology and personal spiritual practice ask different kinds of questions. Philosophical disagreement does not remove standards of valid reasoning; religious and historical empirical claims still require relevant evidence. **Working evidence:** present the strongest form of an argument, objections and replies, with accurate textual references. **Checked guides:** [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/info.html) **Source-checked [R37](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r37)** and [World Religions Today, 7th ed.](https://learninglink.oup.com/access/esposito-wrt7e) **Source-checked [R36](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r36)**. For texts such as the *Tao Te Ching*, identify the translator, source text and editorial approach; compare passages rather than assuming that a publisher label settles accuracy.
