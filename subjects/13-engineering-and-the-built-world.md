## Prepare and practise

Prerequisites vary: calculus and physics support mechanics, thermodynamics, fluids and circuits; statistics supports reliability; drawing and spatial work support design. Analysis, laboratory work, design projects and applicable standards all matter. **Working evidence:** a model with checked assumptions, measurements against predictions, and a design reviewed against requirements. **Checked chemical-engineering foundation:** [Elementary Principles of Chemical Processes, 4th ed.](https://bcs.wiley.com/he-bcs/Books?action=index&bcsId=9771&itemId=1118431227) **Source-checked [R18](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r18)**; structural-analysis alternative [Structural Analysis, 11th ed.](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/pearsonplus/p/9780138026394) **Source-checked [R17](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r17)**.
