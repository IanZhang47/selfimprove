## Prepare and practise

Establish historical and institutional context, research design and statistical literacy. Mathematics becomes more important in economics, demography and formal models. Keep empirical descriptions, causal explanations and normative proposals separate. **Working evidence:** compare explanations against relevant evidence and write a policy analysis with assumptions, tradeoffs and distributional effects. **Checked foundations:** [Introduction to Anthropology](https://openstax.org/books/introduction-anthropology/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R33](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r33)**, [Introduction to Sociology 3e](https://openstax.org/books/introduction-sociology-3e/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R34](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r34)**, [Introduction to Political Science](https://openstax.org/books/introduction-political-science/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R35](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r35)** and [Introducing Public Administration, 10th ed.](https://www.routledge.com/Introducing-Public-Administration/Hyde/p/book/9781032042893) **Source-checked [R41](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r41)**.
