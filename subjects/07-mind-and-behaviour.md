## Prepare and practise

Begin with general psychology and research methods; add measurement, statistics and competing theoretical explanations. A trade book may introduce a question, while a handbook may be a later reference rather than a readable next step. **Working evidence:** distinguish an observation from a proposed mechanism, inspect a study's design, and test a practical technique against an explicit personal outcome. Do not infer universal social rules from one study or population.
