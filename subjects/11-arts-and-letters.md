## Prepare and practise

Learn close reading, historical context and the vocabulary of the medium, while experiencing complete works, performances, recordings and exhibitions. Survey books with Western or other regional scope need complementary traditions. **Working evidence:** an interpretation supported by details of the work, a contextual comparison, or a created work with critique. **Checked literary foundation:** [The Norton Introduction to Literature, 15th ed.](https://seagull.wwnorton.com/introliterature) **Source-checked [R24](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r24)**. Read the [human writings companion](../literature/00-READING-GUIDE.md) for primary works and translation choices.
