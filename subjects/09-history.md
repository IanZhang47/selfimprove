## Prepare and practise

Pair an overview with regional and thematic study, then primary sources with context. General histories are useful maps; scope, documentation and argument must be assessed individually. **Working evidence:** identify authorship, date, audience and survival of sources; compare two historians on a specific question without assuming disagreement makes all explanations equally strong. **Checked global orientation:** [A Concise History of the World, 2nd ed.](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/a-concise-history-of-the-world/5DFC1B45A3C2A2E067F31EA4CCBB1646) **Source-checked [R23](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r23)**.
