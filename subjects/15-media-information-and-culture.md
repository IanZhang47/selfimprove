## Prepare and practise

Learn source tracing, document/media context, measurement and the institutions producing information. Theory and criticism can guide questions; specific factual claims still need verifiable support. **Working evidence:** trace a claim to its origin, check date/context, examine independent evidence, and publish a short verification note with unresolved uncertainties. **Checked practice handbook:** [Journalism, “Fake News” & Disinformation: Handbook for Journalism Education and Training](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/journalism-fake-news-disinformation?hub=67168) **Source-checked [R50](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r50)**.
