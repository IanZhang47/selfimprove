## Prepare and practise

Use demonstrations, progressive projects, observation and feedback alongside manuals. The wrench marks an important practice component; it makes no claim about whether an introductory series covers the topic. Skills develop through performance, and technique books do not prove every accompanying psychological explanation. **Working evidence:** a finished object, meal, design or performance evaluated against explicit criteria. **Checked additions:** [The Professional Chef, 10th ed.](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en?isbn=9781119490951&option=com_eshop&view=product) **Source-checked [R51](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r51)** and [Core: Introduction to Basic Construction Skills, 6th ed.](https://www.nccer.org/craft-catalog/core/) **Source-checked [R52](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r52)**.
