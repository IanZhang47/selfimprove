## Prepare and practise

Combine Earth/life science with chemistry, quantitative reasoning, systems analysis and local context. Distinguish technical feasibility, economics, environmental effects and policy values. **Working evidence:** a field observation record, resource/material balance, map with uncertainty, or analysis of a local system using dated sources. Book examples and numerical estimates may age while their calculation methods remain useful.
