## Prepare and practise

Study evidence and reasoning alongside other subjects. Mathematics branches after arithmetic/algebra, functions and trigonometry: calculus and linear algebra support probability, physics and computing; proof-writing supports analysis, algebra and topology. Begin Axler after basic matrices and proof readiness. **Working evidence:** solve unfamiliar problems, explain assumptions, and independently check one empirical claim. **Checked alternatives:** [Calculus, Volumes 1–3](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R04](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r04)** and [OpenIntro Statistics, 4th ed.](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/) **Source-checked [R06](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r06)**; learning synthesis [How People Learn II: Learners, Contexts, and Cultures](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/24783) **Source-checked [R01](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r01)**.
