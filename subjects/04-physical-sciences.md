## Prepare and practise

Use algebra and calculus appropriate to the chosen route. Mechanics precedes many advanced physics branches; differential equations, multivariable calculus and linear algebra become necessary for deeper physics. Chemistry branches into organic, inorganic, analytical and physical study after general chemistry. **Working evidence:** solve problems and connect predictions with measurements in appropriate laboratory or observation work. **Checked entry alternatives:** [University Physics, Volumes 1–3](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R10](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r10)**, [Chemistry 2e](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R11](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r11)**, [Physical Geology, 2nd ed.](https://opentextbc.ca/physicalgeology2ed/?metadata=book-information) **Source-checked [R13](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r13)** and [Astronomy 2e](https://openstax.org/books/astronomy-2e/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R14](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r14)**.
