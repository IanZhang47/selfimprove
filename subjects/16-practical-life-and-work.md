## Prepare and practise

Connect reading to actual budgeting, collaboration, planning, care, decisions and practical tasks. Record jurisdiction, date and personal assumptions for financial, legal or procedural material. Workplace and relationship advice must fit the people and circumstances involved. **Working evidence:** a usable budget, project plan, negotiation debrief, or repeated skill with feedback. **Checked foundations:** [Your Money, Your Goals: A Financial Empowerment Toolkit](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/your-money-your-goals/toolkit/) **Source-checked [R47](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r47)**, [Principles of Accounting, Volume 1: Financial Accounting](https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R39](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r39)** and [Principles of Management](https://openstax.org/books/principles-management/pages/preface) **Source-checked [R40](../reference/02-CHECKED-LIBRARY.md#r40)**.
